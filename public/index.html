How it works

It's a fair fight

  1. Your image is cut into pieces. Each piece remembers its home position.
  2. The pieces are scrambled once, and every fighter gets the exact same scramble.
  3. Each frame, every fighter gets the same budget of operations: one compare, one read, one swap or one write each.
  4. Whoever puts every piece home in the fewest operations wins. The Headless ms column in the results is real CPU time with the visuals turned off.
